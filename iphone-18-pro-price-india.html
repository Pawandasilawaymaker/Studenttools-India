iPhone 18 Pro Price in India, Specs, Release Date & Features

The iPhone 18 Pro is Apple's latest Pro iPhone and brings upgrades across performance, cameras, display and battery life. If you are searching for the iPhone 18 Pro price in India, release date, storage options and major features, this guide covers the important details in one place.

Apple lists the iPhone 18 Pro starting at ₹1,64,900 in India. Apple says pre-orders begin at 5:30 PM IST on September 12, 2026, with availability starting September 18. 1

iPhone 18 Pro Price in India

The official starting price of the iPhone 18 Pro in India is ₹1,64,900. Apple also offers higher-storage configurations at higher prices.

Because prices, offers and financing options can change, check the official Apple India store before making a purchase.

iPhone 18 Pro Release Date in India

Apple announced that iPhone 18 Pro pre-orders in India begin at 5:30 PM IST on September 12, 2026.

The phone becomes available from September 18, 2026. 2

iPhone 18 Pro Storage Options

The iPhone 18 Pro is available with multiple storage capacities, including:

  • 256GB
  • 512GB
  • 1TB
  • 2TB

The 256GB version is the entry-level storage option, while the 1TB and 2TB versions are aimed at users who need significantly more local storage.

iPhone 18 Pro Key Specifications

  • Display: 6.3-inch class display
  • Refresh rate: 120Hz
  • Processor: Apple A20 Pro
  • Rear cameras: 48MP main + 48MP telephoto + 48MP ultra-wide
  • Front camera: 18MP
  • Operating system: iOS 27
  • Storage: 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB
  • Connectivity: 5G, Wi-Fi and USB-C

Some specifications can vary by region and configuration. The official Apple technical specifications should be treated as the final reference.

iPhone 18 Pro Camera

One of the major upgrades highlighted by Apple is the main camera. The iPhone 18 Pro features a 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture, designed to improve photography in different lighting conditions and provide greater control over depth of field. 3

The Pro camera system is aimed at people who want more flexibility for photography and video rather than simply taking everyday snapshots.

iPhone 18 Pro Performance

The iPhone 18 Pro is powered by Apple's new A20 Pro chip. The processor is designed for demanding smartphone tasks, graphics-intensive applications and Apple's newer AI features.

For buyers upgrading from an older iPhone, performance is one of the main reasons to consider the Pro model.

iPhone 18 Pro Battery Life

Apple highlights improved battery performance on the iPhone 18 Pro generation. The Pro Max model is rated for up to 43 hours of video playback according to Apple's published information. 4

Actual battery life depends on network conditions, brightness, applications, settings and how the phone is used.

iPhone 18 Pro Colours

The iPhone 18 Pro is offered in several finishes, including Black, Silver, Glacier and Burgundy.

Is iPhone 18 Pro Worth Buying?

The iPhone 18 Pro makes the most sense for buyers who specifically want Apple's latest Pro-level camera system, performance and premium features.

If you mainly use a phone for social media, messaging, videos and everyday tasks, a less expensive iPhone may provide better value. Buyers should compare the price difference before choosing the Pro model.

iPhone 18 Pro vs Older iPhone Models

The biggest reason to upgrade from an older iPhone is not simply the new design. The newer Pro model combines a new-generation chip, camera improvements and updated features.

However, if your current iPhone is still fast and receives software updates, waiting for a discount or comparing the previous generation can make more financial sense.
