CGPA to Percentage Formula

A commonly used conversion formula is:

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

For example, if your CGPA is 8.5:

8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%

However, the official conversion formula can vary by university, college or education board. Always check your institution's official guidelines when an exact official percentage is required.
