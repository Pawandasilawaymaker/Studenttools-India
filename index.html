Percentage Calculator

Calculate the percentage of marks you scored out of the total marks.

How this is calculated

Percentage is calculated using the formula:

Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) × 100

For example, if you scored 450 out of 500, your percentage is (450 / 500) × 100 = 90%.

CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Convert your CGPA (on a 10-point scale) into an equivalent percentage.

Most Indian universities (including CBSE) use 9.5. Check your institution's official formula if unsure.

How this is calculated

The most commonly used formula (used by CBSE and many Indian universities) is:

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

For example, a CGPA of 8.4 converts to 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%.

Note: Some universities use a different conversion factor (e.g. 9.0 or 10). Always confirm the exact formula with your institution before using the result for official purposes.

Attendance Calculator

Check your current attendance percentage, and find out how many classes you can safely miss or need to attend.

How this is calculated

Current attendance percentage:

Attendance % = (Classes Attended / Classes Held) × 100

If your attendance is below the required percentage, we calculate how many consecutive future classes you'd need to attend (assuming you attend all of them) to reach the requirement.

If your attendance is already at or above the requirement, we calculate how many future classes you could skip while still staying at or above the requirement (assuming the total number of classes keeps increasing).

SGPA Calculator

Calculate your Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) using subject credits and grade points.

How SGPA is calculated

SGPA is calculated using the credit-weighted average of grade points:

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

Enter the credit and grade point for each subject to calculate your semester SGPA.

Percentage Increase & Decrease Calculator

Calculate the percentage increase or decrease between two numbers.

How it is calculated

Percentage Change = ((New Value - Original Value) / Original Value) × 100

A positive result means an increase, while a negative result means a decrease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Percentage Calculator accurate?

Yes. It uses the standard formula (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks × 100) with no rounding shortcuts, so the result is mathematically exact.

What CGPA-to-percentage formula should I use?

The 9.5 multiplier is the most common formula used by CBSE and many Indian universities, but it is not universal. Some institutions use their own conversion factor. Check your official mark sheet, university handbook, or registrar's office for the exact formula that applies to you.

How does the attendance shortfall/surplus calculation work?

It assumes a simple ongoing scenario: for a shortfall, it estimates how many classes you'd need to attend in a row (with total classes also increasing by one each time) to reach your target percentage. For a surplus, it estimates how many additional classes could be held without you attending, while your percentage stays at or above the target. Actual results may vary slightly depending on your institution's specific attendance policy.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. All calculators run directly in your browser using plain JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent to any server.

Does this website work on mobile?

Yes. The site is built mobile-first, so it works smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktops.
