CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Convert your CGPA (on a 10-point scale) into an equivalent percentage.

Most Indian universities (including CBSE) use 9.5. Check your institution's official formula if unsure.

How this is calculated

The most commonly used formula (used by CBSE and many Indian universities) is:

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

For example, a CGPA of 8.4 converts to 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%.

Note: Some universities use a different conversion factor (e.g. 9.0 or 10). Always confirm the exact formula with your institution before using the result for official purposes.

CGPA to Percentage Calculator for Indian Students

Use this free CGPA to percentage calculator to convert your CGPA into an equivalent percentage quickly. Enter your CGPA and conversion factor to get your result instantly.

How to Convert CGPA to Percentage?

A commonly used conversion formula is: 

Percentage = CGPA × Conversion Factor

Example

If your CGPA is 8.4 and the conversion factor is 9.5: 

8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%

Your calculated percentage is 79.8%.

Important Note for Students

CGPA conversion rules can differ between universities, colleges and education boards. Always check your institution's official conversion formula before using the result for admission, scholarships or other official purposes.

CGPA vs GPA: What Is the Difference?

CGPA usually represents a student's cumulative grade-point performance across multiple semesters, while GPA is a more general term for grade-point average. The exact meaning and calculation method can vary by institution.

SGPA Calculator

Calculate your Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) using subject credits and grade points.

How SGPA is calculated

SGPA is calculated using the credit-weighted average of grade points:

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

Enter the credit and grade point for each subject to calculate your semester SGPA.

SGPA Calculator for College Students

Use this free SGPA calculator to calculate your Semester Grade Point Average using subject credits and grade points.

How is SGPA Calculated?

SGPA is calculated using a credit-weighted average of the grade points earned in each subject. 

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

SGPA helps students understand their academic performance during a semester. Universities may use different grading and conversion rules, so always check your institution's official guidelines when required.

Percentage Increase & Decrease Calculator

Calculate the percentage increase or decrease between two numbers.

How it is calculated

Percentage Change = ((New Value - Original Value) / Original Value) × 100

A positive result means an increase, while a negative result means a decrease.

Percentage Increase and Decrease Calculator

Use this free percentage change calculator to calculate the percentage increase or decrease between an original value and a new value.

How to Calculate Percentage Change?

The standard formula is: 

Percentage Change = ((New Value - Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example of Percentage Increase

If a value increases from 500 to 600: 

((600 - 500) ÷ 500) × 100 = 20%

This means the value increased by 20%.

Example of Percentage Decrease

If a value decreases from 600 to 500: 

((500 - 600) ÷ 600) × 100 = -16.67%

The negative result indicates a decrease of approximately 16.67%.
